The Judgement Deck · The one-page guide

Find your thinnest corner.

Pick one piece of AI-assisted work from this week — the one that mattered. Run it past the five checks below. Be honest: nobody's marking it. The checks that come out "not yet" name your cards.

01 · JUDGETrust

How much should I trust this output?
YESNOT YETI placed each output before acting: how exact must it be, what happens if it's wrong.
YESNOT YETAnything going out the door had every figure, name and date checked from outside the tool.
YESNOT YETI'm most sceptical where I know least, not most confident.
Not yet? Start with cards 01–06

02 · BRIEFThe ask

How do I ask for what I need?
YESNOT YETMy briefs name the audience, the situation and what good looks like — not just the task.
YESNOT YETThe standing context is saved once, not retyped into every chat.
YESNOT YETWhen output keeps missing, I add an upgrade (+ Example, + Done) before rewriting from scratch.
Not yet? Start with cards 07–13

03 · CHECKVerification

How do I know it's right?
YESNOT YETBefore any analysis, I asked it to describe the data first: rows, columns, gaps.
YESNOT YETEvery number that could change a decision got recounted by me, not by the tool.
YESNOT YETThe deliverable itself says what I checked and how.
Not yet? Start with cards 14–19

04 · DELIVERThe fit

Will it work every week?
YESNOT YETThe workflow has one owner, one success measure, and a baseline recorded before we started.
YESNOT YETEvery amber or red step has a named person and a specific check.
YESNOT YETThere's a gate date with scale, pivot and kill criteria, set before the demo convinced anyone.
Not yet? Start with cards 20–26

05 · PRACTISEThe reps

Is my judgement getting sharper?
YESNOT YETOnce a week, on a task that matters, I do a rough pass myself before reading AI's.
YESNOT YETOne decision of mine is written as if-then rules and has been tested blind.
YESNOT YETI could name, out loud, what I would never let the tool decide.
Not yet? Start with cards 27–30
