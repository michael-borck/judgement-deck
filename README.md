# The Judgement Deck

Fifty cards for using and delivering AI with judgement, distilled from the Human
Edge masterclass and companion book. Free product: no gate, no capture.

**Direct it. Don't delegate. Never surrender.**

## deck/

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `judgement-deck-50-cards.md` | Full deck, markdown. 10 Judge, 11 Brief, 10 Check, 11 Deliver, 8 Practise. 30 habit cards + 20 brief cards. |
| `judgement-deck-50-print.html` | Full deck, print & cut. 7 A4 pages, 8 cards per sheet, cover card first. |
| `judgement-deck-30-cards.md` | Essential cut, markdown. 6 Judge, 7 Brief, 6 Check, 7 Deliver, 4 Practise. |
| `judgement-deck-30-print.html` | Essential cut, print & cut. 4 A4 pages. |
| `judgement-desk-card.html` | One A4 pin-up: the whole deck in four panels. Print at 100%, heavier stock if available. |
| `judgement-deck-guide.html` | One-page diagnostic: run one piece of real work past five checks; the "not yet"s name your cards. |
| `fonts/` | Domine, Funnel Sans, Nanum Gothic Coding (SIL OFL), copied from the course assets. |

## The pairing

1. **The guide** diagnoses (one page, five checks).
2. **The deck** treats (pull the cards your "not yet"s named).
3. **The desk card** stays (the four moves, after the cards are memorised).

## Print setup

A4, scale 100% / actual size, background graphics on. Print the desk card and
guide single-sided; the deck sheets cut along the dashed lines.
